Inside the Michael Lane Edition

From the first note
to the final master.

The core Lemon Studio workflow, expanded with instruments, expressive editing, recording tools and a mixer designed to feel like a studio.

Updated in build 2026110302Included · Instruments / Synth

Lemon Wave

A dual-oscillator wavetable and recorded-sample instrument, with a dedicated sound browser, drawable modulation and original factory sounds. Build a patch from familiar waves, import your own tables, or play a recorded synth note across the keyboard.

Choose your sound

Six named basic waves, four wavetable families, periodic WAV import and a grouped My Samples browser. Sample root-pitch controls and embedded patch audio keep your chosen sounds with your project.

Draw the movement

Four drawable 65-point LFOs, eight modulation routes, four macros, drag-to-assign modulation, dotted/triplet sync divisions and stage/playhead feedback.

Shape the tone

Unison, sub and noise sources, curved AHDSR envelopes, Classic/SVF 12/SVF 24 filtering, chorus and delay. Fourteen factory choices and 109 stable host controls.

My Samples uses your own local catalogue and recordings; third-party sample libraries are not bundled. Recorded notes play as individual pitched samples, not automatic multisample zones. Sound/root edits may interrupt held notes; make those edits between notes.

01Compose & perform
  • Pattern sequencer, Channel Rack, Playlist and Piano Roll for building complete arrangements.
  • Native harmony and chord workspace with Simple and Complex views and instrument audition.
  • Scale Notes Only supports 22 scales in all 12 keys; filtered notes remain intact.
  • Instrument layers, typing-keyboard playback, channel Mute/Solo and selected-note Move/Copy.
  • Per-note MPE pitch, pressure and slide editing and recording, expressive MIDI import/export, and lower/upper zones.
  • Tap tempo, precise decimal BPM entry, slower tempo dragging, tempo undo and global pitch controls.
02Record & comp
  • Mixer-input recording with monitoring through inserts, buses, effects and the master.
  • Simultaneous input recording, track arming and independent take solo.
  • Non-destructive take lanes and comping with saved selections and undo/redo.
  • Automatic MIDI and automation capture when no mixer audio input is armed.
  • Comp edits stay independent of automation cuts; recording overflow preserves elapsed time as silence with a warning.
03Edit audio & arrange
  • Clip gain, fades, slicing, Make Unique and waveform-detail controls.
  • Manual warp markers and pitch-preserving stretching with source preservation and undo.
  • Independent pattern-clip stretching and Make it fit length choices.
  • Reversible track Freeze/Unfreeze, track Bounce and channel Consolidate/Bounce to Audio.
  • Effects-rendered and keep-mixer-live bounce options retain the original; grouped track families move together.
  • Named marker profiles with save, merge and replace, plus subdued marker colors.
04Three ways to mix
  • Full analog-style mixer, traditional compact mixer and original-plugin channel-strip mixer.
  • Native low cut, gate, drive, EQ, resonance taming and compression; Tube, Tape, Soft and Clip drive styles.
  • Vocal layouts with pitch controls and de-essing, plus independent strip bypass.
  • Dedicated EQ with spectrum, Pre/Post routing and its own enable control.
  • Analog VU and stereo meters, grouped faders, section jump controls and illuminated polarity/swap buttons.
  • Per-channel and global Effects bypass, expandable slots and drag reordering.
05Original Waves channel strips
  • The manufacturer’s original plugin interface sits above the mixer fader.
  • Supported focus: Scheps Omni Channel, SSL E/G Channel, SSL EV2, Abbey Road TG12345, Magma and Renaissance Channel.
  • Section-aligned paging for wider interfaces, including TG12345; resizable-editor fitting.
  • Fill channels with the same strip or choose a different strip for each channel.
  • Drag channels onto the plugin panel or fader area to route them.
  • Waves plugins require your own installation and licence. Other manufacturers are not the tested focus.
06Master & monitor
  • Integrated Lemon Master Assistant with mastering EQ, dynamic EQ, multiband compression, stereo imaging and maximizer.
  • Dedicated master controls with settings stored in the project.
  • Tape-style Varispeed changes the complete mix’s pitch and duration together, including exports.
  • Monitor-only headphone correction with searchable AutoEq profiles; exports remain unaffected.
  • Signed automatic updates and matching editable source are available for each platform.
07Lemon Chop & sample tools
  • Lemon Chop slicing instrument with cue pads, automatic slicing, editable regions and pad names/favorites.
  • Reverse, pitch and stretch controls, undo/redo and saved project state.
  • Optional local stem separation with selectable stems or combinations and source-region export.
  • Sample preview, drag/drop loading, sampler-to-mixer routing and a compact routing picker.
  • Browser preview-only Normalize/Reverse and sample-dialog audition.
  • Tempo estimation from sample length is available; it is not automatic rhythm detection.
08Effects & automation
  • Independent audio Clip Effects chains with bypass, copy/paste, parameter automation and recall.
  • Built-in synths, samplers, EQ, dynamics, modulation, delay, reverb and utility effects organized on first launch.
  • CLAP, VST3 and VST2 hosting, subject to the installed plugin and platform.
  • Transparent automation overlays, curve selection and a separate visibility switch.
  • Automation for native strip controls, sends and slot mix, with saved baselines and Return to original value.
  • One-shot automation capture and an optional Parameters browser tab for plugin automation.
09Routing & workflow
  • Mixer buses, sends and Quick Sidechain, with routing selection from sample controls and clip menus.
  • Drag/drop routing adopts Playlist names/colors and supports undo.
  • Mixer channel insertion and copy/paste include processing and outgoing routes; channel moves retarget automation.
  • Independent mixer-chain multicore processing and plugin delay compensation.
  • Customizable shortcuts, focused-editor key handling and independent plugin windows for multi-monitor work.
  • Native .lmn projects, supported FLP/FST import paths and matching source archives. Third-party FL plugin compatibility is not universal.
10Browser & community
  • Samples and Favorites with recursive search, previews and nested folders.
  • Editable instrument/effect databases, a searchable visual plugin picker and favorites.
  • Optional Single Folder View, browser tabs, auto-hide and a Parameters tab hidden by default.
  • Curated plugin downloads, preview cards and supported portable installation.
  • Integrated feature voting, community forum and searchable Change Log.
  • Native starter templates, optional Starter Pack installation and an empty personal sample library for new users.

Before you install

A clear view of compatibility.

Bring your plugins

Third-party plugins need their own licences and compatible platform builds. Original Waves interfaces are the current channel-strip mixer focus. MPE requires a compatible instrument and patch mapping; physical controllers and every patch have not been tested.

Platform & project support

Intel Mac checks run under Rosetta, not physical Intel hardware. Linux packages are compiled, not runtime-tested. ARA integration is present, but Auto-Tune ARA compatibility and Melodyne transport issues remain. FLP/FST import does not guarantee every FL Studio plugin or saved setting will transfer.

Make it yours.

Download the editable source, build instructions and licence notices. Every platform has matching source archives. This edition is customized by Michael Lane, based on Lemon Studio by ItsAlphaNeon and contributors.
